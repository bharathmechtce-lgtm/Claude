## 1. Introduction
Indian Insights Company ("IIC", "we", "us", or "our") operates a WhatsApp-based ordering assistant ("Order Bot", "the Bot", or "the Service") that enables customers to place orders via WhatsApp chat, which are processed and entered into your connected ERP system (e.g., SAP).
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect your personal information when you interact with our WhatsApp Order Assistant Bot. By using the Bot, you consent to the practices described in this policy.
This policy is published in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) of India.
---
## 2. Information We Collect
### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
When you interact with the Order Bot via WhatsApp, we may collect:
- **Contact Information:** Your WhatsApp phone number, name, and profile information as provided by WhatsApp Business API.
- **Order Details:** Product names, quantities, delivery addresses, preferred delivery dates, and any special instructions you provide during the ordering process.
- **Business Information:** Company name, GST number, billing address, and other business-related details necessary for order processing.
- **Communication Content:** Chat messages, voice notes (if applicable), and any files or images shared during the ordering conversation.
### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Chat Metadata:** Timestamps, message delivery status, and session identifiers.
- **Interaction Data:** Conversation flow patterns, bot command usage, and order completion rates (collected in aggregate for service improvement).
- **Device Information:** Device type and WhatsApp version (as provided by the WhatsApp Business API).
### 2.3 Information from Third-Party Systems
- **ERP System Data:** Order history, account status, credit limits, product catalogs, and pricing information retrieved from the connected ERP system (e.g., SAP) to fulfill your orders.
---
## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- **Order Processing:** To receive, validate, confirm, and enter orders into the ERP system on your behalf.
- **Order Fulfillment:** To communicate order status, delivery updates, and resolve order-related queries.
- **Customer Support:** To respond to your inquiries, troubleshoot issues, and provide assistance related to your orders.
- **Service Improvement:** To analyze usage patterns (in aggregate and anonymized form) to improve bot accuracy, response quality, and overall user experience.
- **Business Communication:** To send order confirmations, invoices, delivery notifications, and other transactional messages.
- **Compliance:** To maintain records as required by applicable tax, accounting, and business regulations.
---
## 4. Data Storage and Security
### 4.1 Data Storage
- Order data is stored in the connected ERP system (e.g., SAP) as maintained by the subscribing business.
- Chat logs and conversation data are retained on secure cloud infrastructure hosted in India or in compliance with data localization requirements under applicable Indian law.
- WhatsApp message data is also subject to WhatsApp's own data retention policies.
### 4.2 Data Retention
- **Chat Logs:** Retained for a period of 12 months from the date of the last interaction, after which they are automatically deleted unless required for legal or regulatory compliance.
- **Order Data:** Retained in the ERP system as per the subscribing business's data retention policies and applicable statutory requirements (e.g., GST records for the statutory period).
- **Aggregate Analytics:** Anonymized and aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for service improvement.
### 4.3 Security Measures
We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures, including:
- End-to-end encryption provided by the WhatsApp Business API for message transmission.
- Encrypted storage for data at rest.
- Role-based access controls to limit data access to authorized personnel only.
- Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
- Secure API connections between the Bot and ERP systems using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS).
---
## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:
- **ERP System Integration:** Order data is transmitted to the subscribing business's ERP system (e.g., SAP) for order processing and fulfillment. The subscribing business is the data controller for this data.
- **Service Providers:** With trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the Bot (e.g., cloud hosting providers, AI/NLP processing services), subject to strict data processing agreements.
- **WhatsApp/Meta:** Your use of WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy. Message metadata is processed by WhatsApp/Meta in accordance with their terms.
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
- **Business Transfers:** In connection with any merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of the business, subject to the same privacy protections.
---
## 6. AI and Automated Processing
### 6.1 How AI is Used
The Order Bot uses artificial intelligence and natural language processing (NLP) to:
- Understand your order requests from natural language chat inputs, including Hinglish (Hindi-English mixed language) conversations.
- Extract order details such as product names, quantities, and delivery preferences.
- Validate orders against the ERP system's product catalog and pricing.
- Generate order confirmations and respond to queries.
### 6.2 Human Oversight
- Complex or ambiguous orders may be flagged for human review before being entered into the ERP system.
- You may request human assistance at any point during the conversation.
- Automated decisions related to order validation (e.g., credit limit checks) are based on rules configured by the subscribing business in the ERP system.

## 7. Your Rights
Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and other applicable laws, you have the following rights:
- **Right to Access:** You may request a summary of your personal data that we process.
- **Right to Correction:** You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
- **Right to Erasure:** You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** You may withdraw consent for data processing at any time by sending "STOP" or "OPT OUT" to the Bot, or by contacting us at the details below. Withdrawal of consent may affect our ability to provide the ordering service.
- **Right to Grievance Redressal:** You have the right to file a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India if you are unsatisfied with our handling of your data.
- **Right to Nominate:** You may nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity, as provided under the DPDPA.
To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 11.
